  • Industries come from each company's SEC SIC code mapped to the Fama-French 49 industries. A company filed under a broad code can land in a surprising group. The SIC description is shown so you can check.
  • "Stock vs industry" is the stock's 12-month wind (latest month skipped) minus its S&P 500 industry peers, weighted by size. The marker shows where it ranks among all S&P 500 stocks on the same measure. A quick 2021–2026 test on today's members found the top 20% beat the bottom 20% by about 6 points over the next 6 months, measured against their industries. That test is short and has survivor bias, so this column doesn't change the action yet.
  • Tax estimates use 32% short-term and 15% long-term rates (change with --st-rate / --lt-rate). They ignore state tax, the net investment income tax and lots not in the file.
  • Position limit 10%, industry limit 25% (change with --position-cap / --industry-cap).
